About WaW Image Viewer

      

        
An unofficial tool made for the World at War modding community.

        

          Browse the IWI images inside your game's main IWD archives. Search by
          filename, inspect image channels and cubemap faces, copy names, or
          save original IWIs and PNG copies.
        

        

          This displays stored images, not in-game materials or lighting. Game
          archives are never modified. Thumbnails are cached locally and can be
          cleared at any time.
        

        

          Independent of Treyarch, Activision and UGX. Built with JavaScript,
          Vite, Rust and Tauri. Open source under GPL-3.0. Wavelet decoding is
          adapted from OpenAssetTools; see ATTRIBUTION.md.
        

        
Created by phil81334.

        
        
        

          
        

      

    
    
      
Thumbnail cache

      

        

          Thumbnails and the archive index are stored here. Full-size previews
          stay in memory. Clearing the cache does not change your game or
          exported images.
        

        
        

          Archive size and modification time are checked at startup. Rescan
          forces a fresh read if you replace an archive while keeping those
          values.
